## Overview

This research explores the integration of Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) for flood assessment and resilience planning. These technologies can support geospatial analysis, flood-risk assessment, identification of potentially vulnerable areas, and data-driven disaster preparedness.

## Research Significance

Integrating AI/ML techniques with GIS offers a way to combine data-driven analysis with geographic information. Such an approach can support flood-risk evaluation and help inform resilience planning and disaster management decisions.
